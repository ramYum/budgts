Logo

A squared spiral, built on a grid: your spending, tracked inward turn by turn to a single solid point. One corner is cut at 45° — the mark is closing on the total, not sealing shut.

Type

Two families. Poppins for anything that carries the brand's voice; Inter for everything you read and every number you check.

Budgts on track Raygo 012·£3,480

Poppins — SemiBold 600, Bold 700. The wordmark, headings, big balances, empty-state lines. Geometric and round, tracked slightly tight (-3 to -5%).

Inter — Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700. Body copy, labels, buttons, tables. Every currency amount uses Inter with tabular figures so columns line up.

Set body text at 16 px with generous leading, measures under 64 characters, and left aligned. Sentence case everywhere — headings included. No all-caps, no letter-spaced labels.

Voice

Clear. Calm. On your side.

We write

You've got £120 left for groceries this month.

Couldn't reach your bank. Nothing was lost — your manual entries are all here.

Not

Uh oh! You're about to blow your grocery budget 😬

Error 502: upstream sync failure. Please try again later.

State the number and what it means. Don't scold, don't celebrate loudly, don't apologise for the software. An empty screen says what to do next, not "nothing here yet".
